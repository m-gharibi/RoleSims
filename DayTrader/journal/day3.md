## THE THREE QUESTIONS

Answer these last, in full sentences, and do not be generous with yourself.

**1. What did I do today that I had NOT planned to do — and why?**
Go through Part 1 line by line and find every deviation. For each: what was the
state of the world in the ten seconds before I deviated? Was it new information,
or was it a feeling that arrived with a story attached?

**2. With this exact same tape tomorrow, what would I do differently?**
Be concrete: different entry, different size, different stop, different number of
trades. And separately — which decisions would I keep even though they lost?

**3. What did today teach me about myself under pressure?**
Not about the market. About me. What is my characteristic move when the number is
red — do I freeze, get bigger, get busy, or get out too early? Did it show up
more than once? Has it shown up in a previous session? Where else in my life does
this exact move appear?
