## PART 5 — THE RETRO NARRATIVE

**Write this before submitting. Before the score. Before the reveal.**

The sim asks you for a narrative and for what you would do differently. Draft both
here, in full, while you still do not know the answer. Tobias's actual question was:
*"What do you know now that you wish you'd known on Monday?"*

### The narrative

Four to six paragraphs. What you committed to, what you did, what you got wrong and
when you knew, what you would keep. **No hedging and no pre-emptive apology** — you
are describing decisions made under uncertainty by someone who could not see the
answer, which is the only kind of decision anyone ever makes.

Things a good narrative contains and a bad one does not:

- A number in nearly every paragraph.
- At least one decision you would make again despite the outcome, named as such.
- At least one decision that worked out and that you would not repeat.
- The specific day you knew something, rather than "eventually it became clear."
- What it cost other people, priced.

>

### What I would do differently

Not a list of five aspirations. Two or three mechanisms, each testable by someone
reading the log.

>

### The one thing I know now that I wish I'd known on Monday

One sentence. Not about the codebase — the codebase is invented and the sentence
will be worthless in a week if it is about DST handling. About how you work when you
cannot see the bottom.

>
