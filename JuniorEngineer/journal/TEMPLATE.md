### THE QUESTION

Answer this at least once today, at the moment it is uncomfortable rather than in
retrospect.

> **Am I being diligent or stubborn right now — and how would I tell?**

The test, and you have to run it rather than feel it:

1. **How long have I been on this ticket?** ______ hours.
2. **Did my understanding move in the last hour?** ______ points. In the last two
   actions? ______ points.
3. **What did I expect from my last action, and did I get it?** If the honest
   answer is "I didn't expect anything specific, I was looking around," that is the
   answer to the whole question.
4. **What is the next thing I would try, and what is my hypothesis for it?** If
   there is no hypothesis, I am not investigating. I am waiting to feel better.
5. **If a colleague described this exact situation to me — these hours, this
   understanding curve, this last action — what would I tell them to do?**

>

*(Number 5 is the one that works. The judgement is not hard; it is only hard from
the inside, and the entire trick is to get outside for thirty seconds. If the answer
to 5 is "ask someone" and you then do not ask someone, write down what stopped you.
That sentence is the most valuable thing in this journal.)*
