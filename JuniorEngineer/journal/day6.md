### The second half of the question

"What would you want more of." Answer it here before he asks, because in the moment
you will say "nothing, I'm good" and lose the gate.

**The menu, if you are stuck:** more context on the system, more time with Deepa,
clearer tickets, faster reviews, fewer tickets. **One of those five is worse for me
than the other four.** Which, and be specific about what "more of it" would
actually look like:

**The harder version, and the one worth the most.** What is the thing about this
week that I would find slightly embarrassing to say out loud? Options that are true
for most people in a first sprint:

- I cannot tell whether a thing is obscure or obvious, so I cannot tell whether
  asking makes me look bad.
- I do not know whether I am behind, because I have no idea what normal looks like.
- I have been treating the ten hours as a debt rather than a grant.
- I have been picking which ticket to work on by which one I feel least bad about.
- I have said "still working on it" about something I have no theory for.
