# DECISION LOG — Week 12 | The QBR, written before the score

**Everything above the line in this file is written before you submit the QBR and
before any score, grade or truth is revealed.** That constraint is the entire
value of the document. A narrative written after the number is a narrative fitted
to the number, and you will not be able to tell the difference from the inside —
which is precisely the thing this exercise exists to demonstrate.

Twenty minutes. Do not scroll to the bottom.

---

# PART A — WRITTEN BEFORE THE REVEAL

## A1. THE NARRATIVE

Five paragraphs, in this order. This is the thing you would say standing up in a
room, and it is what you will paste into chat.

**1. The number and what it did.** Baseline, where it landed, the gap to the
mandate. One sentence, no cushioning, no burying it in paragraph three.

>

**2. What I decided and why.** The prioritisation principle, stated as the rule
it was, and the set it produced. Not a list of features — the rule, and then the
features as a consequence of the rule. If the rule changed during the quarter,
say when and what changed it.

>

**3. What I gave up, and to whom.** Named. With what each person was told and
when. Include the ones where the honest answer is "I told them late."

>

**4. What I got wrong.** Separate three things that are usually smeared
together: beliefs about impact that were wrong; beliefs about cost that were
wrong; and decisions that were badly made regardless of how they turned out. The
third category is the only one that is actually about you.

>

**5. What I would do differently, specifically.** Not "research more." A
different decision, at a nameable week, that follows from something I now know.

>
