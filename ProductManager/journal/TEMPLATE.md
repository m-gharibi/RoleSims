# DECISION LOG — Week ___ | Day ___ | ______

This is a decision log, not a status report. Nothing in it describes what
happened; everything in it records what you decided, on what basis, and what you
gave up. Status is in the sim. This is the part the sim cannot hold.

Fill it **before** the corresponding chat gate. Filling it afterwards lets you
launder the reasoning into whatever the room reacted well to, which destroys the
only evidence you have about your own judgment.

Ten to fifteen minutes. Never edit an earlier week's entry — strike it through
and write the new line underneath, so the change is visible.
